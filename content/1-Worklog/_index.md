Throughout my **8-week internship** at **First Cloud AI Journey**, I had the opportunity to explore and work with a wide range of Amazon Web Services (AWS), progressing from fundamental cloud concepts to advanced deployment, automation, and cloud operations.

Each week was organized around specific learning objectives that combined theoretical knowledge with hands-on laboratory exercises. In addition to completing the assigned training modules, I documented the tasks performed, knowledge gained, practical skills developed, and challenges encountered throughout the internship.

The weekly Worklog is organized as follows:

- **Week 1:** [AWS Fundamentals, IAM, Billing, and AWS Budgets](1.1-Week1/)
- **Week 2:** [Amazon S3, EC2, RDS, and High Availability](1.2-Week2/)
- **Week 3:** [CloudWatch, Hybrid DNS, and AWS CLI Automation](1.3-Week3/)
- **Week 4:** [High Availability Architecture and Migration to AWS](1.4-Week4/)
- **Week 5:** [Infrastructure as Code, CloudFormation, and Operational Automation](1.5-Week5/)
- **Week 6:** [Docker, Amazon ECR, Amazon ECS/EKS, and Kubernetes](1.6-Week6/)
- **Week 7:** [DevOps, GitHub Actions, CI/CD, and Application Deployment](1.7-Week7/)
- **Week 8:** [Final Project Deployment, Monitoring, Cost Optimization, and Internship Summary](1.8-Week8/)

> **Overview:** This Worklog documents my learning journey throughout the internship, covering AWS fundamentals, hands-on labs, cloud infrastructure deployment, system monitoring, automation, containerization, and DevOps practices. It demonstrates the progress I made, the technical skills I developed, and the practical experience I gained while designing, deploying, and managing solutions on AWS.
