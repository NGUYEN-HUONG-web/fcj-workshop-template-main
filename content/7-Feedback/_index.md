### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is very friendly and open. FCAJ members are always willing to help whenever I encounter difficulties, even outside working hours. The workspace is tidy and comfortable, helping me focus better. However, I think it would be nice to have more social gatherings or team bonding activities to strengthen relationships.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentor provides very detailed guidance, explains clearly when I don’t understand, and always encourages me to ask questions. The admin team supports administrative tasks, provides necessary documents, and creates favorable conditions for me to work effectively. I especially appreciate that the mentor allows me to try and solve problems myself instead of just giving the answer.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The tasks I was assigned align well with the knowledge I learned at university, while also introducing me to new areas I had never encountered before. This allowed me to both strengthen my foundational knowledge and gain practical skills.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
During the internship, I learned many new skills such as using project management tools, teamwork skills, and professional communication in a corporate environment. The mentor also shared valuable real-world experiences that helped me better plan my career path.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The company culture is very positive: everyone respects each other, works seriously but still keeps things enjoyable. When there are urgent projects, everyone works together and supports one another regardless of their position. This made me feel like a real part of the team, even as an intern.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The company provides an internship allowance and offers flexible working hours when needed. In addition, having the opportunity to join internal training sessions is a big plus.

### Additional Questions
**What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**

The most satisfying aspect was the opportunity to work on a real, meaningful project (Slothub) from start to finish. Being able to see how all the pieces fit together - from infrastructure setup to application development to deployment - was incredibly rewarding. Additionally, the supportive learning environment where I could ask questions freely and learn from experienced professionals made the experience very fulfilling.

**What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**

The program is already excellent, but I think it could be enhanced with more structured learning paths for interns with different backgrounds. For example, interns coming from AI/ML backgrounds (like myself) might benefit from more introductory sessions on cloud computing and software development fundamentals before diving into complex projects. Additionally, more peer learning sessions or intern networking opportunities could be valuable.

**If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?**

Absolutely, I would highly recommend this internship program to friends. It’s an excellent opportunity to learn cutting-edge technologies, work on real projects, and gain exposure to enterprise-level practices. The supportive environment, experienced mentors, and hands-on learning approach make it ideal for anyone looking to grow their skills in cloud computing, DevOps, or software development. The program is particularly valuable for students who want to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industry practice.

### Suggestions & Expectations

**Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**

Consider providing more structured onboarding sessions covering AWS fundamentals for interns with limited cloud experience
Organize more technical workshops or hands-on labs on specific topics (e.g., Terraform, Lambda functions, API Gateway)
Create opportunities for interns to present their work or share learnings with the team
Consider pairing interns with different skill levels for peer learning
Would you like to continue this program in the future?
Yes, I would be very interested in continuing with the program if the opportunity arises. The learning experience has been excellent, and I believe there’s still much more to learn, especially as I continue to develop my cloud and software development skills.

**Any other comments (free sharing):**

This internship at AWS FCJ has been a transformative experience for me. Coming in with a strong background in AI/ML but limited experience in cloud and software development, I was able to significantly expand my skill set and gain confidence in new areas. The opportunity to work on the Slothub project, learn from experienced mentors, and experience a professional working environment has been invaluable. I’m grateful for the supportive culture, the learning opportunities, and the chance to contribute to a real project. This experience has not only enhanced my technical skills but also helped me understand how to work effectively in a team and communicate professionally in a corporate setting. I would encourage any student interested in cloud computing, DevOps, or software development to apply for this program.
